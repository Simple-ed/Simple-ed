# Subject Line Swipe File

## Principles
- Keep it under 6 words when possible
- No clickbait -- be direct and relevant
- Lowercase feels more personal (test both)
- Personalization in subject lines increases open rates

---

## By Category

### Pain-Point Driven
- `updating models after earnings`
- `manual data entry from filings`
- `your {{sector}} models`
- `model updates during earnings season`
- `hours spent on data entry`

### Curiosity / Question-Based
- `quick question about your workflow`
- `how does your team update models?`
- `thought about {{firm_name}}'s research process`
- `question for the {{firm_name}} team`

### Value-Led
- `5-10 hours back per week`
- `one-click Excel model updates`
- `13 years of data, one click`
- `faster models, fewer errors`

### Social Proof / Authority
- `what {{similar_firm}} is doing differently`
- `used by teams like yours`
- `how top analysts are saving time`

### Trigger-Based / Timely
- `before earnings season`
- `congrats on the new hire`
- `saw {{firm_name}} in the news`
- `post-earnings thought`
- `re: your data vendor review`

### Breakup / Final Touch
- `should I close the loop?`
- `not sure if this landed`
- `last note from me`
- `closing the loop`

---

## A/B Testing Notes

Test these variables:
1. **Personalized vs. generic** -- e.g., "your AAPL model" vs. "model updates"
2. **Lowercase vs. title case** -- e.g., "quick question" vs. "Quick Question"
3. **With firm name vs. without** -- e.g., "thought for {{firm_name}}" vs. "thought for your team"
4. **Short (2-3 words) vs. medium (4-6 words)** -- e.g., "your models" vs. "updating models after earnings"

Track open rates per variant and double down on winners after 50+ sends per variant.
