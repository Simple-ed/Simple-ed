# Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP)

## Primary Personas

### 1. Hedge Fund Equity Analyst
- **Title**: Equity Research Analyst, Senior Analyst, Portfolio Manager
- **Firm type**: Long/short equity, fundamental hedge funds, multi-strategy funds
- **AUM**: $500M - $50B+
- **Day-to-day pain**: Spending 3-5+ hours per earnings season manually pulling numbers from 10-Qs, 10-Ks, earnings releases, and investor presentations into Excel models
- **What they care about**: Speed to update models after earnings, data accuracy, getting an edge before the market moves
- **Trigger events**: Earnings season (especially heavy coverage weeks), new analyst hire ramp-up, expanding coverage universe
- **Example firms**: Point72, Citadel, Millennium, Balyasny, DE Shaw, Two Sigma Advisors

### 2. Private Equity Associate / VP
- **Title**: Associate, Senior Associate, VP
- **Firm type**: Growth equity, buyout, PE firms running deal pipelines
- **AUM**: $1B - $100B+
- **Day-to-day pain**: Building models from scratch during due diligence, manually sourcing historical data across 10+ years of filings
- **What they care about**: Faster due diligence, comprehensive historical data, accuracy for IC memos
- **Trigger events**: Active deal pipeline, new fund raise, expanding sector coverage
- **Example firms**: KKR, Blackstone, Silver Lake, Vista Equity, Thoma Bravo

### 3. Investment Banking Analyst / Associate
- **Title**: Analyst, Associate, VP in Coverage or M&A groups
- **Firm type**: Bulge bracket, elite boutique, middle-market banks
- **Day-to-day pain**: Building comps, precedent transactions, and DCF models under tight deadlines; manually updating pitchbook data
- **What they care about**: Speed (everything is urgent), accuracy for client-facing materials, reducing late nights
- **Trigger events**: Live deal mandates, pitchbook season, new analyst class onboarding
- **Example firms**: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan, Evercore, Lazard, Centerview

### 4. Research Director / Head of Research
- **Title**: Director of Research, Head of Equity Research, CIO
- **Firm type**: Asset managers, hedge funds, sell-side research departments
- **Day-to-day pain**: Team productivity bottlenecks, inconsistent data across analysts' models, slow ramp time for new hires
- **What they care about**: Team-wide efficiency, standardized data quality, reducing operational overhead
- **Trigger events**: Hiring new analysts, expanding coverage, evaluating data vendor contracts (renewal season)
- **Example firms**: Any buy-side or sell-side firm with 5+ analysts

---

## Qualifying Questions

Before reaching out, confirm:
1. Does the prospect's firm rely on fundamental equity research?
2. Do they build/maintain financial models in Excel?
3. Is the firm large enough to benefit from automation (typically 3+ analysts)?
4. Are they in a coverage-heavy period (earnings season, deal flow)?

## Disqualifying Signals
- Purely quant/systematic strategies (no fundamental modeling)
- Firms that exclusively use third-party models (no in-house modeling)
- Very small firms with 1-2 people who don't maintain detailed models
