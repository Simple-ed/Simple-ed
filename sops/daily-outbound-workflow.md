# Daily Outbound Workflow SOP

## Daily Schedule (2-4 hours)

### Morning Block (Recommended: 8-10 AM)

#### 1. Inbox Review (15 min)
- [ ] Check for replies from previous outreach
- [ ] Respond to warm replies immediately (priority #1)
- [ ] Log all responses in CRM/tracker
- [ ] Schedule any meetings requested

#### 2. LinkedIn Engagement (15 min)
- [ ] Check LinkedIn notifications and messages
- [ ] Respond to any connection accepts/messages
- [ ] Engage with 5-10 posts from target prospects (like, comment)
- [ ] Post content if scheduled

#### 3. New Outreach (60-90 min)
- [ ] Send new email sequences (target: 30-50/day)
- [ ] Send LinkedIn connection requests (target: 20-30/day)
- [ ] Make cold calls if applicable (target: 20-30 dials)

---

### Afternoon Block (Recommended: 2-4 PM)

#### 4. Follow-ups (30 min)
- [ ] Send follow-up emails to non-responders
- [ ] Send LinkedIn follow-up messages
- [ ] Leave voicemails for no-answers

#### 5. List Building (30 min)
- [ ] Research and add new prospects
- [ ] Verify email addresses
- [ ] Add personalization notes
- [ ] Ensure pipeline stays full (always 1 week ahead)

#### 6. End of Day Review (15 min)
- [ ] Update tracking spreadsheet/CRM
- [ ] Note what worked/didn't work
- [ ] Plan tomorrow's priority targets
- [ ] Celebrate wins!

---

## Weekly Goals

| Activity | Daily Target | Weekly Target |
|----------|--------------|---------------|

| LinkedIn Requests | 20-30 | 100-150 |

| Follow-ups | 20-30 | 100-150 |

## Quality Standards

### Every outreach must have:
1. **Personalization** - Reference something specific about them
2. **Relevance** - Clear reason you're reaching out to THEM
3. **Value** - What's in it for them?
4. **Clear CTA** - One specific ask

### Never:
- Send generic mass emails
- Pitch too early on LinkedIn
- Be pushy or desperate
- Lie or exaggerate
- Spam (follow CAN-SPAM, GDPR if applicable)

---

## When to Stop Outreach

Stop contacting a prospect when:
- They explicitly ask to stop
- They've been through full sequence with no response
- They're clearly not a fit
- 3+ months since last engagement (re-add to nurture)
