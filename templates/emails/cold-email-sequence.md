# Cold Email Sequence Templates

## Variables
- `{{first_name}}` - Prospect's first name
- `{{company}}` - Prospect's company name
- `{{pain_point}}` - Specific pain point relevant to their industry
- `{{result}}` - Specific result/outcome you can deliver
- `{{case_study}}` - Brief mention of relevant case study

---

## Email 1: Initial Outreach (Day 1)

**Subject Lines (A/B test):**
- Quick question about {{company}}'s growth
- {{first_name}}, noticed something about {{company}}
- Idea for {{company}}

**Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{company}} is [specific observation about their business].

We help companies like yours {{result}} by [brief value prop].

Would it make sense to chat for 15 minutes this week?

Best,
[Your name]
```

---

## Email 2: Follow-up with Value (Day 3)

**Subject:** Re: [previous subject]

**Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Quick follow-up - wanted to share something relevant.

{{case_study}} - we helped them achieve [specific result] in [timeframe].

Happy to walk you through how we did it if useful.

[Your name]
```

---

## Email 3: Different Angle (Day 7)

**Subject:** {{pain_point}} at {{company}}?

**Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Many [their role/industry] I speak with are dealing with {{pain_point}}.

If that resonates, I have a few ideas that might help.

Open to a quick call?

[Your name]
```

---

## Email 4: Breakup Email (Day 14)

**Subject:** Should I close your file?

**Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

I haven't heard back so I'll assume the timing isn't right.

No hard feelings - I'll close out my notes on {{company}} for now.

If things change, feel free to reach out anytime.

Best,
[Your name]
```

---

## Best Practices

1. **Keep it short** - Under 100 words per email
2. **Personalize** - Reference something specific about them/their company
3. **One CTA** - Don't give multiple options
4. **Send timing** - Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am or 4-6pm local time
5. **Follow up** - Most replies come from follow-ups, not first emails
