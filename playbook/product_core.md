# Daloopa Core Product

---

## Overview
Daloopa is a fundamental data and automation platform that lets you build, audit, and update financial models using the most complete historical dataset for public equities. The Excel Add-In pulls in figures from the latest earnings and press releases into models instantly, no formatting needed.

## Coverage
- **5,000+ tickers**
- **47 industry comp sheets**
- **99%+ quarterly average accuracy rate**

## Data Sources
- SEC Filings
- Press Releases
- Investor Presentations
- Footnotes
- Select Transcripts

## Differentiated Datapoints Captured
- GAAP and Non-GAAP Disclosures
- Company Disclosed Guidance and KPIs
- Granular Segment and Geographic Breakdowns

---

## Daloopa Hub (Datasheet & Document Portal)
Houses all historical datasheets for 5,000+ tickers.

### Features:
- **Company Earnings Page:** Historical source documents, newly disclosed data and restatements all in one place, with latest updates
- **Datasheet downloads** for 5,000+ tickers and 47 industry comp sheets

---

## Daloopa Excel Add-In
Allows analysts to update their models with the click of a button whenever new disclosures are released.

### Features:
- Updates match native formatting, accommodate most time-period formats
- Hardcoded inputs with no assumptions or adjustments
- Hyperlinks and sources overlaying each data point
- Simple and one-time onboarding process to get models update-ready
