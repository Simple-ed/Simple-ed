# Daloopa Prospecting Playbook
## Source: Internal Training Notes (Updated March 2024)

---

## Purpose
Understand how we effectively prospect into target accounts. Reach the right people to sell into accounts at the fastest rate — Champions, Decision Makers, and Evaluation Teams.

---

## Target Contact Criteria

### Must Have:
- Account "Business Type" = **Hedge Fund**
- Account "Named Account" = **Yes**

### Target Job Titles (Fundamental / L-S Equity):
- Analyst
- Associate
- Associate Portfolio Manager
- Equities
- Equity
- Head Of Sector
- Investor
- L/S
- Partner
- Portfolio Manager
- Principal
- Research Analyst
- Senior Analyst
- Senior Associate

### EXCLUDED from Standard Targeting:
- **Quant** (any title containing "Quant")
- **Macro** (any title containing "Macro")

> These personas require a differentiated approach — see Quant/Macro sequence.

---

## Contact Priority Framework

### First Priority: Senior Analysts supporting the PM
- End user with some influence on buying process
- Needs approval from PM/data team
- Senior Analysts have influence; Juniors and Associates typically do not

### Second Priority: Portfolio Manager
- Usually a decision maker (controls team resources)
- Not directly the end user

### Third Priority: Data Team / Director of Research supporting PM's sector
- Controls central budget and compliance
- Growing trend: they want to slash budgets and limit vendor count
- At major funds, prospects will tell us to reach out to internal data team

### Don't Forget: Executive who understands fund's strategic goals
- CIOs with analyst backgrounds
- Principals
- Can intro us to the right people

## BDR Positioning by Persona

### First Priority — Analysts:
- Pitch use cases
- Leverage modeling/data quality pain points they're familiar with
- Find out sector coverage and workflow information
- Ask to include anyone else on the meeting who would be relevant

### Second Priority — Portfolio Managers:
- Pitch use cases
- Leverage modeling/data quality pain points they're familiar with
- Find out sector coverage and workflow information
- Ask to include anyone else on the meeting who would be relevant

### Third Priority — Data Team / Director of Research:
- Budget and compliance angle
- Vendor consolidation narrative
- Scalability of coverage without headcount
