# Analyst Schedule & Prospecting Timing Strategy

---

## Typical Analyst Workday

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | Usually in office |
| 8:30 AM - 11:00 AM | Active market hours / morning work |
| 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM | **"Downtime"** — dedicated focus time (no market news during trading hours) |
| 2:00 PM - 6:00 PM | Afternoon market hours / work |
| 6:00 PM | Senior analysts leave desk |
| 7:30 PM | Junior analysts leave desk |

**Key Insight:** Companies don't report news during market hours, creating a natural "downtime" window from 11 AM - 2 PM when analysts sit down and focus.

**Fridays:** Companies do not report on Fridays, making the entire day essentially "downtime."

**Earnings Season Reality:** During earnings season, analysts are NOT sleeping much. They're working nights and weekends updating models. Monday mornings are already underwater — they walked in buried. **Weekends** are when they're catching up and more likely to notice your email or LinkedIn message. Don't assume a normal schedule during earnings.

**LinkedIn:** NOT viewed at work — no one wants to be suspected of job hunting. **Weekends are prime LinkedIn time** — analysts browse at home, not at the desk.

## Channel Strategy & Timing

### Cold Calls & Demos
- **Best window: 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM** (downtime)
- Sometimes slightly before or after this window
- Fridays are also good for calls

### LinkedIn InMail & Posts
- **Best served OUTSIDE of office hours** (before 8:30 AM or after 6:00 PM)
- **Weekends are the best window** — analysts browse LinkedIn at home, not at the desk
- Not required outside hours, but significantly better engagement
- During earnings season, Saturday/Sunday morning is prime time

### Email
- Analysts skim through ~1,000 emails per day
- Subject lines must earn the open — use curiosity, personalization, or a relevant question
- Keep subject lines short (3-7 words), lowercase, conversational
- Reference something specific to the prospect (their sector, their firm, their workflow)
- Never lead with your company name in the subject line — that signals a sales email and gets skipped

## Apollo Sequence Timing Recommendations

### Email Steps (Auto & Manual)
- **Weekday sends:** 7:00-8:30 AM ET (top of inbox on arrival) or 6:00-7:00 PM ET (less noise)
- **Weekend sends (earnings season):** Saturday 8:00-10:00 AM ET or Sunday 6:00-8:00 PM ET — these analysts are working, and their inbox is quieter
- **Avoid Monday morning** — they're already buried from the weekend. Your email gets lost in the pile.
- Subject lines should be short, curiosity-driven, and personalized — never lead with your company name
- Optimize for open rate: reference the prospect's firm, sector, or a relevant pain point

### Phone Call Tasks
- Set call task windows for **11:00 AM - 2:00 PM ET**
- Fridays are bonus call days (full "downtime")
- Avoid early morning (pre-market prep) and late afternoon (market close)

### LinkedIn Steps
- **Weekends are #1** — Saturday and Sunday mornings are the highest-engagement window
- Weekday fallback: before 8:30 AM or after 6:00 PM
- Connection requests > InMails during off-hours
- During earnings season, weekend LinkedIn activity spikes — analysts are home but still thinking about work
